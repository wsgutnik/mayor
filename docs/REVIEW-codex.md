1. [sim.ts:250](/Volumes/T7/07-Projects/clones/src/sim/sim.ts:250), **high** — Bulldozing one tile removes every road segment containing that tile, but stops are removed only from the clicked tile. Scenario: place stops at both ends of one long segment, then bulldoze a third tile; both stops survive on empty ground, their lines survive, and `updateCoverage()` continues granting transit coverage. Smallest fix: collect all tiles whose segments will be removed and call `removeStopAt` for every stop on any tile that no longer has a road after raster rebuilding. **Confidence: high.**

2. [transit.ts:156](/Volumes/T7/07-Projects/clones/src/sim/transit.ts:156), **high** — Ridership and fare calculations never verify that bus-line legs remain reachable. Scenario: create a valid bus line, bulldoze its only connecting road, and leave the stop tiles intact; buses disappear after failing to route, but the line continues producing riders, income, transit share, and reduced car spawning. Smallest fix: on road-topology changes, prune/disable bus lines with an unreachable leg, or skip such lines in `updateTransit`. **Confidence: high.**

3. [sim.ts:337](/Volumes/T7/07-Projects/clones/src/sim/sim.ts:337), **medium** — Power and water demand are counted only for buildings already inside the corresponding coverage. There is also no `noPower`/`noWater` penalty, so uncovered buildings vanish from shortage calculations. Scenario: half a city loses power coverage; reported demand falls instead of showing a shortage, and happiness receives no direct power penalty. Smallest fix: always add each building’s utility demand, separately count missing coverage, and include missing-power/water penalties as is already done for sewage and garbage. **Confidence: high.**

[traffic.ts:116](/Volumes/T7/07-Projects/clones/src/sim/traffic.ts:116), **medium** — Every A* call allocates and initializes map-sized `Float32Array` and `Int32Array` buffers plus growing heap arrays. Spawning can invoke this repeatedly per frame, and line creation allocates another full zero-load array. At size 128, frequent spawning creates substantial allocation/GC pressure. Smallest fix: keep reusable pathfinding scratch buffers on `TrafficState`, using generation stamps or a touched-index list to avoid full fills. **Confidence: high.**

5. [sim.ts:397](/Volumes/T7/07-Projects/clones/src/sim/sim.ts:397), **medium** — Connectivity is deferred until the next daily step, while traffic runs every animation frame. Scenario: place or bulldoze a road while paused; `tickTraffic` refreshes its lists because `revision` changed but reads stale `connected`, so new access roads cannot spawn traffic and demolished access roads can still be selected until time advances. Smallest fix: resolve `connectivityDirty` before traffic uses connectivity, ideally at the start of `tickTraffic`, or update it immediately after road mutation. **Confidence: high.**

6. [sim.ts:400](/Volumes/T7/07-Projects/clones/src/sim/sim.ts:400), **medium** — Land value is recomputed only every five days and before that day’s building changes. Facilities, road connectivity changes, bulldozing, and new/removed industrial buildings therefore leave `landValue`, the HUD average, and tax revenue stale for as many as five days. Scenario: bulldoze a park immediately after a day divisible by five; its land-value bonus persists through four more revenue calculations. Smallest fix: introduce `landValueDirty`, set it for coverage/connectivity/zone/level changes, and recompute before `recount`. **Confidence: high.**

[scene.ts:248](/Volumes/T7/07-Projects/clones/src/render/scene.ts:248), **medium** — Every world revision performs a full terrain update and normal recomputation, rebuilds all road/transit geometry, scans every tile, and rewrites all instances—even when only one building level changed. Scenario: an entry at `(0, 5)` with `dx=-1` passes validation; `entryRoadTiles` produces negative indices, and leveling/raster setup operates on invalid tiles. Smallest fix: require a cardinal nonzero direction and validate all three generated coordinates with `inBounds` before using them. **Confidence: high.**

Scenario: on a 128×128 map filled with one zone and level, the two deterministic variants are unlikely to split exactly 8,192/8,192; whichever exceeds 8,192 loses visible buildings despite their simulation presence. Facilities similarly disappear after `size²/4` instances of one kind. Smallest fix: size each mesh for the worst case, or count required instances before constructing/resizing meshes. **Confidence: high.**

13. [world.ts:274](/Volumes/T7/07-Projects/clones/src/sim/world.ts:274), **low** — `nearWater` ignores positions outside the map, so map-edge land does not count as adjacent to water. Scenario: a coastline represented by the world boundary has dry edge tiles but no in-bounds submerged tile within three cells; pumps and sewage outlets are rejected despite being placed on the shore. Smallest fix: treat out-of-bounds neighbors as water if the intended map model is surrounded by water, or use the baked water mask. **Confidence: medium; this depends on the intended boundary model.**
