## Product

Mayor is an original single-player city builder (inspired by the genre, no third-party game assets) in TypeScript,
Three.js and Vite with a pure TypeScript simulation. pt-BR by default, English available. It runs offline.

What a player gets today: Line/Curve road drawing with snapping and angle guides (street, avenue, highway, one-way,
roundabout, bridge, tunnel, overpass, interchange, upgrade in place), junctions with sidewalks, crosswalks and lane
arrows; street-aligned R/C/I zoning up to 4 cells deep, lots that evolve from 1x1 to 4x4, 8 rare landmarks; power,
water and sewage carried along roads, civic services with coverage, 84 catalog buildings (models for 1-40, x2/x4
evolved models, 7 new service models; most of 41-84 use the procedural fallback), service buildings that snap to the
street and show usage numbers; traffic with IDM car-following, priority rules, adaptive signals and gridlock
watchdogs; buses, metro and rail; demographics, construction stages, statistics; day/night with HDRI, lit windows,
light pools, volumetric clouds and per-biome ground textures; three save slots, autosave, import/export; touch
controls for iPad; About and credits screen.

Tests: **968 passed, 3 skipped, 2 todo, 117 files** (`npx vitest run --exclude '.claude/**'`); `npx tsc --noEmit`
clean. Measured: zone reconcile on the 96x96 fixture 7.3 ms per road edit, 6.0 ms per bulldoze, 2.7 ms per
2000-tile repaint (Mac, 2026-10-15; was 46-188 ms before `cf1d484`). Real iPad, Windows and Mac frame-time profiling:
not measured.
