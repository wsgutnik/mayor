## 1. Scale and Grid

- **[source]** 1 unit = 1 m; one zoning grid cell = 8 m × 8 m; recommended floor height 2.5-3 m. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Asset_Editor
- **[source]** "divided into small 8×8 m squares"; "Areas up to four squares from a road can be zoned" (≈32 m). — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Zoning
- **[source]** One map tile is 1.92 × 1.92 km; the map is 5×5 tiles, 9 purchasable, 92.16 km² playable (Colossal Order, cited by press). — https://pcgamer.com/cities-skylines-2-map-size
- **[source]** 1081 × 1081 px heightmap. — https://github.com/cspiewak/RandomCities and https://steamcommunity.com/app/255710/discussions/0/611701360841488365
- **[inference]** 1081 px over 9 × 1.92 km = 17.28 km gives ≈16 m per terrain pixel, so terrain has half the zoning-grid resolution (8 m).
- **[inference] Application to Mayor:** Mayor's tile (1 unit) corresponds to the CS1 8 m cell. A 96 × 96 tile map is 768 m per side, less than half a CS1 tile; 128 × 128 is ≈1 km. CS1 road widths (2-4 cells) are 2-4× wider than Mayor's street (0.9 tile).

## 2. Road Network: Segments, Nodes, Lanes

- **[source]** "Routes consist of one or more Segments; Segments contain one or more Lanes; Segments are joined together by Nodes"; every segment has a node at each end even when not connected to another segment; segments can curve and rise between the two nodes. — TM:PE wiki, https://github.com/CitiesSkylinesMods/TMPE/wiki/Nodes,-Segments,-Lanes (search snippet)
- **[source]** In the Road Editor, **segments** are used on continuous road and **nodes** at intersections, transitions between road types, and endpoints; both are "straight stubs" that the game bends and connects. Meshes are "usually modeled as straight 64 units long and 16-32 units wide"; `Half Width` and `Segment Length` (usually 64) come from properties; widths below 8 cause problems. Sidewalk height is 0.3 units, sidewalk level y = 0, asphalt slightly lower. Nodes need a single edge per side between sidewalks at the end, otherwise terrain holes appear. `Min Corner Offset` above 40 breaks collision. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Road_Editor
- **[source]** Road textures: diffuse `_d`, normal `_n`, specular `_s`, alpha `_a` (grass in Basic shader), pavement `_p`, and road `_r` (white areas generate automatic texture). "Avoid diffuse details on heavily stretched nodes". Lanes: position, width, vertical offset, types None/Vehicle/Pedestrian/Parking/CargoVehicle/TransportVehicle; lane props (poles, traffic lights) with lateral position, height, offset, `Repeat Distance`, and `Probability`. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Road_Editor
- **[source]** Widths: Small Roads tab roads and ramps = 2 cells; 2- and 3-lane highways = 3 cells; the rest = 4 cells. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Roads
- **[source]** Traffic lights: 2-lane road does not generate lights; 4-lane generates them at most intersections; 6-lane at all except one-way leaving it; highway does not generate with one-way. Player toggles traffic light and stop sign in the "Junctions" info view. Roundabouts are made with 2-lane road and have no lights. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Roads
- **[source]** One-way: traffic goes from the initial point to the final point of the drawn road; right-click in Upgrade mode reverses it. Tools: Straight, Curved, Freeform, Upgrade; snapping to roads, angles, zoning grid, and guidelines; elevation step 12 m (3/6/12). — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Roads and manual (`CitiesSkylines-UserManual_EN_djvu.txt`, "Roads": "option to make roads snap to zone grid"; "The road Is built and the zoning grid appears next to the road")
- **[source]** Roads without decoration have side parking; decorations (grass/trees) reduce noise and raise land value. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Roads
- **[source]** Game limits (2015 post on limits, via search): Net Segments 32,768, Net Nodes 32,768, Net Lanes 262,144, Zoned Blocks 32,768. Exact URL not confirmed (result pointed to https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/531738447/481115363859488292).
- **[source] CS2:** every road except highway carries 40 MW low voltage and, except bridges, water/sewage pipes; roundabouts in 4 sizes (₡200-1,500); modes Straight/Simple Curve/Complex Curve/Continuous/Grid/Replace; parallels with offset 0.25 to 12 cells; snapping every 8 m; elevation steps 1.25/2.5/5/10 m. — https://cs2.paradoxwikis.com/Roads
- **[inference] Application to Mayor:** Mayor has only segments (`RoadSegment` with 2-3 points) rasterized into tiles; there is no node. Corners, intersections, traffic lights, stop signs, and crosswalks in CS are properties of the **node**; without a node, there is nowhere to attach them.

## 3. Zoning, Blocks, and Lots

- **[source]** Zoning only exists beside a road that allows zoning; the grid appears when the road is built; depth up to 4 cells (32 m); building front "immediately adjacent to the road". Sizes 1×1 to 4×4 cells (growables max 4×4). Fill, marquee, and brush tools (2 sizes). De-zoning replaces buildings gradually. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Zoning, https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Asset_Editor, manual ("Zoning")
- **[source]** Densities: residential and commercial have low/high; industry and office do not. Households per cell (low residential, levels 1-5): ≈0.2 / 0.25 / 0.333 / 0.375 / 0.4166; high residential: 0.6 / 1.0 / 1.33 / 1.5 / ≈1.6; industrial jobs per cell 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Zoning
- **[source]** Building level: residential depends on land value and education; commercial on land value and customer wealth; industry/office on worker education and service coverage. — manual and Zoning wiki
- **[source]** Community (Steam forum): 8×8-cell blocks allow four 4×4 buildings; widening the road shrinks the block to 6×6, so 10×10 is recommended if widening later; for dense areas, 13 cells between medium roads. — https://steamcommunity.com/app/255710/discussions/0/611701360840502796
- **[source]** CS2: one guide recommends a 112 × 88 m block (https://dotesports.com/general/news/cities-skylines-2-optimal-grid-size-guide); another shows that 12-cell spacing is the maximum without a hole in the middle (https://steamah.com/cities-skylines-ii-practical-engineering-efficient-grids-guide/).
- **[source]** Services: "Most city service buildings need to be built on zones next to roads"; when choosing the building, roads turn green (covered) or red. — manual, "City Service Buildings"
- **[inference]** In CS1 the zoning grid is generated **per segment, per side**, aligned to the segment direction, not the world grid: on curved roads cells follow the curve, and at corners the blocks from both segments overlap and the game resolves cell ownership. Corner lots are not a special type: a 4×4 building on a corner simply fronts the road whose block owns it. This is not written in any source read; it is the model used by zoning mods (Zoning Adjuster) and visible in game.
- **[inference] Application to Mayor:** today zoning is 1 tile = 1 building, paintable anywhere; nothing defines a "block". What the player calls a block in CS is the area enclosed by roads, with 4-cell strips on each side and a middle core (or not) in the center.

## 4. Water, Sewage, Power, and Underground View

- **[source]** Pipes: ₡20/cell, ₡0.08/week; a single network carries water and sewage; a building is served if it is **up to 11 cells** from a working pipe; pipes do not block tunnels/metro. Pumping station 3×5 cells, 120,000 m³/week, near water; water tower 2×2 on any land; sewage drain 2×7, 120,000 m³/week, pollutes water; treatment plant 8×9 cuts 85%. Water has flow direction: pump upstream from drain. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Water_and_sewage
- **[source]** Power: buildings form a network among themselves "over short distances"; lines (₡20/cell, ₡0.48/week) connect separated networks and must be placed within blue areas; Page Up/Down changes height. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Electricity and manual ("Electricity Grid & Power Lines")
- **[source]** Info views: Electricity shows connected buildings in blue with pale-blue reach area; **Water shows currents and flow direction, water buildings, and underground pipes** (and allows pipe demolition); Heating is similar for heating pipes; Traffic paints loaded roads red; Education/Police paint roads green (covered) or gray; Land value, Levels, Pollution (water pollution follows current), Districts with policy icons. 36-37 info views. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Info_views and manual ("Info Views")
- **[source]** Underground view: with road/rail/metro tab open, Page Down lowers the level until underground view appears; since Mass Transit there is a button next to bulldozer and the **V** key toggles above/below ground; traffic overlay helps see tunnels; Toggle It mod notes that "tunnels and pipes are tied to info views". Up to three underground road levels. — https://steamcommunity.com/app/255710/discussions/0/133256240731245305, https://steamcommunity.com/app/255710/discussions/0/615085406656384206, https://steamcommunity.com/app/255710/discussions/0/3187988461099013321, https://www.dedoimedo.com/games/cities-skylines-underground.html
- **[source] CS2:** low-voltage cables and pipes come automatically with roads; cables connect to transformers, which connect to high-voltage lines and power plants. Road pipes cannot be removed without removing the road; there is an "Underground Mode" in the bulldozer tab. — https://www.simplegamer.co.uk/?p=21589, https://twinfinite.net/guides/how-remove-underground-pipes-cities-skylines-2/
- **[inference]** Visually, CS1 underground view makes the terrain translucent/darker, keeps roads as reference, and draws pipes (pale blue) and tunnels as tubes at ground level with blue coverage around them. No source read describes the rendering in words; this is what is visible in game.
- **[inference] Application to Mayor:** radius coverage (`FACILITY_INFO.radius`) has no network; underground view needs a per-tile pipe/cable network to show. The CS2 model (pipes and cables come with roads, manual construction only from intake point to network) is simplest to implement and explain to the player.

## 5. Water: Simulation and Appearance

- **[source]** "the first integrated Dynamic Water System giving all the rivers and estuaries within the game real-world mass and direction"; sewage flows downstream and can contaminate intake. — https://www.dsogaming.com/news/cities-skylines-final-dev-diary-focuses-on-in-game-tools/
- **[source]** Water info view shows currents and flow direction; water pollution follows current. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Info_views
- **Not obtained:** no source with colors, foam, or water-shader resolution for CS1. Water Color Changer mods only confirm that water color is configurable RGB 0-1. — https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/comments/435723412
- **[inference]** What makes CS water look like water: shoreline at terrain level (plane × terrain intersection, not a per-cell mask), color by depth, animated normals with sun specular, sky reflection by Fresnel, foam only in shallow bands and strong current, and wet/darkened terrain under the sheet. Standard techniques described at https://gamedev.net/shaderlab/53-foam-with-flow-mapping/ and GDC 2012 "Water Technology of Uncharted" (https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/water-technologyofunchartedgdc2012/12977478).

## 6. Assets, Textures, LOD, and Budget

- **[source]** Triangles: simple building < 1,000, large ≈ 5,000, exceptions 10-15k; vehicles 500-1,000; trees up to ≈2,000. LOD: growables ≈100 tris, props/vehicles <50, LOD texture 128 × 128; trees become billboards automatically. Textures: 1024 × 1024 standard (1024 × 512 for small), 2048 × 1024 for large, 2048² only for huge; maps `_d _n _s _a _c _i` (illumination: 120 = full, 128-255 = random windows). Ploppable lots up to 16 × 8 cells. — https://skylines.paradoxwikis.com/Asset_Editor
- **[source] CS2:** graphics options include shadow quality, terrain casting shadows, level of detail (default 70%), SMAA/TAA, depth of field and tilt-shift, volumetric clouds; the official guide says to disable DoF to gain FPS. — https://support.paradoxplaza.com/hc/en-us/articles/14648080779538-Cities-Skylines-2-Our-Guide-to-Optimizing-Performance, https://www.ggrecon.com/guides/cities-skylines-2-best-graphics-settings-improve-performance-fps
- **[inference] Application to Mayor:** Mayor's shared textures are 128 × 128 (asphalt, grass) and 256 × 256 (brick), with a 64 × 64 window atlas; the CS kit uses 1024² per building. Mayor has no LOD or shadows. For iPad, the path is shared 512-1024 textures in atlases, 2-level LOD per instance, one-cascade shadow, and post-processing only in the high tier.
